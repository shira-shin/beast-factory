# ヴァルガ：作曲・音楽生成用プロンプト

## 追加曲：既存5曲から曲調を変える（2026-11-13）

以下はそれぞれ単独で貼り付けるプロンプトです。下の旧「共通指定」は付けないでください。全曲にチェロ・ピアノ・金属打楽器を指定すると、また似た音になります。まず1・2・4の三方向から作ると差が出ます。

### A. 通常戦・軽快な読み合い「獣の駆け引き」

Instrumental tactical battle music, 138 BPM, acoustic breakbeat jazz, nimble upright bass, dry live drum kit with ghost notes, short baritone sax phrases, occasional muted trumpet answers. Start with a distinctive bass-and-drums groove in the first two seconds. A sly, dangerous duel, agile rather than comedic. Write a memorable six-bar melodic idea answered by two bars of silence. Three contrasting 16-bar sections; the middle section drops the horns for bass and rim clicks, then returns with a new counter-rhythm. 80–100 seconds, loop-friendly ending with no final hit or fade. No vocals, orchestra, cello, prepared piano, choir, cinematic booms, synth arpeggios, risers or EDM drops. Keep horns out of the foreground during drum accents; leave space for impacts. Natural dynamics, unclipped transients, clear mono playback.

### B. 通常戦・俊敏な魔獣「夜走り」

Instrumental dark breakbeat battle loop, 164 BPM, chopped dry funk drums, elastic analog mono bass, dub chords, short granular glass stabs. Half-time spacious passages alternate with rolling broken rhythms; rhythmically complex but only three layers at once. No four-on-the-floor kick. A nocturnal hunt through an abandoned foundry, quick decisions and sudden openings. An immediately recognizable two-bar drum signature; a separate three-note bass hook, varied rather than repeated unchanged. 90 seconds, three sections, drums begin immediately, last bar leads smoothly into the opening groove, no intro or outro. No vocals, strings, brass, acoustic piano, choir, trailer effects, constant distortion or build-and-drop structure. Restrained sub-bass, clear on phone speakers, leave the upper midrange open for combat effects.

### C. 2対2・連携「四つの足音」

Instrumental battle music, 116 BPM, Afro-Latin percussion ensemble with electric bass, marimba and restrained rhythm guitar. Two distinct motifs pass between marimba and guitar like partners trading attacks. A simple bass anchor supports interlocking conga, rim and shaker patterns; never fill every beat. Earthy, tense and confident, not tropical holiday music. In section B, one motif shifts against the pulse; in section C both resolve in a brief unison before separating. A 96-second loop with an immediate groove, no long entrance, no victory ending. No vocals, orchestral strings, saxophone, synth pads, epic drums or cinematic risers. Small dry room, expressive human timing, space for four combatants' sounds.

### D. 強敵・重量級「顎鉄」

Instrumental progressive groove-metal battle track, 104 BPM, low-tuned electric guitar, gritty picked electric bass, tight dry acoustic drum kit. Heavy syncopation and deliberate rests, not constant double-kick drumming. One short angular guitar riff, a contrasting bass-led section, then a restrained upper-register guitar melody above the riff. Weight comes from locked timing and silence, not loudness. A dangerous colossal beast that waits before striking. 90–120 seconds, start with the core rhythm immediately; loop back without a final crash or fade. No vocals, choir, orchestra, ambient intro, trailer booms, cymbal wash, guitar solo shredding or wall-of-sound limiting. Bass notes remain readable on small speakers; leave transient space for heavy impacts.

### E. 異変・天候戦「空の裂け目」

Instrumental spectral battle loop, 92 BPM, plucked koto-like zither, struck stone, breathy low flute, a sparse pulse of hand drums. Modal pentatonic melody with one unsettling outside note; no conventional heroic chord progression. The feeling of reading an opponent under changing wind and strange light. A clear steady pulse supports turn-based decisions. Sparse first section, an exposed flute answer, then a more rhythmic zither variation; no continuous arpeggio. 75–100 seconds, seamless energy at the loop boundary, no fade-out. No vocals, orchestra, piano, giant taiko ensemble, choir, fantasy tavern clichés or recorded weather effects. Let the game's weather sound remain audible; wide spaces and short reverberation.

### F. 放置観戦・長時間用「工廠の深夜便」

Instrumental downtempo dub-jazz groove, 96 BPM, warm electric bass, brushed dry drums, occasional vibraphone and very sparse electric guitar harmonics. Suspenseful and slightly strange, calm focus rather than sleep music. An understated four-bar groove with small changes every eight bars; a distinct contrasting middle section, no endless identical loop. Avoid sharp high-frequency instruments and loud peaks. A two-minute loop, starts with the groove, ending returns naturally without fading. No vocals, lead saxophone, orchestral strings, piano ostinatos, cinematic build, vinyl crackle, rain recordings or EDM drop. Keep the music behind creature calls and attack sounds. A controlled low end and a clear mono mix for phone speakers.

制作時は「短い導入・終わりのフェードなし」で依頼してください。ただし生成だけで継ぎ目が消えるとは限らないので、完成音源の拍・残響を聴いてループ位置を編集します。音量・ループ点は実ファイルを受け取ってから揃えます。推奨受け渡しはWAVまたは高音質MP3、曲名にA〜Fの用途を添えてください。

---

狙いは「魔獣と契約する、煤けた工廠」。同じ音色と短い主題を共有し、曲ごとの編曲で戦況を変えます。既存作品や作曲家の模倣を指定せず、過剰な予告編音楽や常時全力のミックスを避けます。

## 共通指定（各プロンプトの末尾に追加）

Instrumental game soundtrack, no vocals or spoken words. A small, tactile ensemble: bowed cello, prepared upright piano, muted low strings, dry hand-played drums, restrained metallic percussion. Audible dynamics, deliberate rests, a clear bass line, short room reverb, a focused stereo image that remains clear in mono. Leave space for combat sound effects. Avoid trailer braams, giant choir, EDM drops, endless risers, glossy synth arpeggios, constant cymbal washes, and a brickwall-limited master. Original composition. Use a short D–A–C–E-flat motif as a recognizable recurring idea, not a continuous ostinato.

## 1. タイトル／契約「炉の眠り」

78 BPM, D minor with occasional Phrygian color. The night shift in an ancient beast foundry. Sparse prepared-piano notes, a warm bowed-cello phrase, a quiet, irregular pulse from wooden and iron percussion. Uneasy, intimate, dignified; curiosity rather than horror. Introduce the four-note motif only twice. Let silence and decaying notes carry the atmosphere. A 90–120 second instrumental loop, a short entrance, restrained variation, no dramatic climax, and a loop-friendly ending at the same energy and harmony as the beginning.

## 2. 通常戦／1対1「刻印の間合い」

124 BPM, 4/4, D minor. A tense duel between two powerful beasts in a ruined foundry. Tight hand-played toms, clipped low-string figures, a grounded bass line, prepared-piano counter-rhythms, occasional scraped-metal accents. Give each phrase a clear attack, a short answering phrase, and a breath. Confidence and danger without heroic triumph. Rework the four-note motif into a restrained cello hook. A 90-second loop with two related sections; rhythm starts immediately, no long intro, no drop, no final cadence. Keep the midrange open for impacts and spell effects.

## 3. 2対2「双獣の交響」

132 BPM, 4/4. Two contrasting voices learn to fight together. Low cello plays a deliberate three-note question; a dry prepared piano answers with an agile syncopated phrase. Interlocking percussion suggests coordinated attacks, while the bass stays simple and readable. At the middle section, combine the voices into one brief, satisfying unison, then return to the dialogue. Dark industrial chamber music with human timing and physical texture. A 90–120 second loop, no cinematic introduction, stable pulse, no crowded lead instruments, no constant climax.

## 4. 番人／決戦「工廠の主炉」

144 BPM, D Phrygian, 4/4 with occasional displaced accents. An enormous sealed beast wakes beneath the foundry. Deep bowed strings, forceful but dry drums, heavy low-piano attacks, and sparse iron strikes. Develop the same four-note motif from a quiet threat into a stern, memorable melody. Weight comes from rhythm, register, and silence rather than sheer loudness. Two clear sections with controlled escalation and one short exposed cello passage. A two-minute battle loop; strong opening pulse, no long build-up, no choir, no trailer explosions, no triumphant ending.

## 5. 窮地「残り火の呼吸」

152 BPM, 4/4. A wounded beast steadies its breathing and watches for one opening. The normal battle theme reduced to a dry pulse, a tense bass pedal, short cello fragments, and widely spaced piano strikes. Urgent but focused, never a wall of sound. Keep deliberate gaps for counterattacks and ultimate animations. No heartbeat recording or alarm sound. A 45–60 second instrumental loop, immediate rhythmic clarity, a subtle rise in tension, loop ending matches the opening.

## 6. 勝利「封印、再び」

An original 9-second instrumental victory cue. A single restrained iron strike, then the D–A–C–E-flat motif transformed into a warm D-minor-to-D-major resolution, played by cello and prepared piano. A short drum answer and a natural room decay. Relief earned after a hard duel, understated pride, no crowd, no brass fanfare, no vocals. One complete musical sentence, clear ending, no loop.

## 7. 敗北「消えぬ契約」

An original 12-second instrumental defeat cue. A low cello holds one vulnerable note while the prepared piano quietly plays a broken version of the main motif. One soft wooden percussion accent, then an unresolved but gentle ending. Weariness and the possibility of trying again, not melodramatic tragedy. Sparse, intimate, no choir, no rising effects, no sudden loud hits. Natural short decay, no loop.

## 納品・差し替え

- まずステレオWAVの原本を保存。ブラウザー用はMP3またはAACへ圧縮し、スマホのスピーカーでも主題と低音の輪郭を確認します。
- 「ループ対応」の指定だけで継ぎ目は保証されません。最後の拍と先頭の拍、残響のつながりを編集ソフトで確認してください。
- ループ曲は同程度の聴感音量に揃え、奥義・破勢・反応の効果音が埋もれないようにします。
- この版は提供BGM5曲を使用しています。別の曲を作ったら `assets/music/` に置き、`js/audio-bank.js` の `music` 内の `file` を対応させます。
- 対応キー：タイトル=`title`、契約=`shop`、偵察=`scout`、通常戦=`battle`/`battle2`、窮地=`danger`、番人=`gate`、決戦=`boss`。双獣試闘の専用曲は `duo`。未指定なら通常戦の曲を使います。
- 勝利後は `music.victory`（loop: false）。敗北の短い音は `sfx.defeat` の配列を差し替えます。
- 差し替え後、親フォルダーから `node reforged/build.cjs` を実行すると画像・音を含む単一HTMLを再生成します。
